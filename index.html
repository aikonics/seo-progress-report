Website search progress – SEO Quartz

How often each website shows up in Google, and how many people click through. Last 12 months, 16 Nov 2025 – 15 Nov 2026.

Source: Google Search Console (Aik's account), data pulled 18 Nov 2026. November 2025 and November 2026 are half months.

Summary

  • Informpros is the bright spot. 200 visits from Google in the last 3 months and rising every month.
  • Four early-learning sites fell sharply in July (Bank Street, Waldorf, Reggio Emilia, HighScope). Bank Street, Waldorf and Reggio Emilia are down 55–92% and their visits have mostly dried up. These sites share near-identical pages with each other, which Google tends to filter out.
  • The float and infrared sites are seen but not clicked. Google shows them thousands of times a month, but on page 5–9 of results, where hardly anyone looks.
  • Google only keeps a small share of each site's pages (8–38%, except Informpros at 54%). Pages Google skips can't bring visitors. This is the same issue blocking AdSense approval.
  • The Ayurvedic sites and City Oasis are too new to judge.

How to read this table
Status
Growing: more visits and shown more often. Seen, not clicked: Google shows the site a lot, but too far down the results for people to click. Flat: little change. Dropping: big fall since July. New: too early to judge.
Visits May–Jul → Aug–Oct
Total visits from Google in May–July (left) and in August–October (right, bold). If the right number is bigger, the site is improving. Example: 27 → 37 means 27 visits, then 37. If both numbers are under about 10, the site gets almost no Google traffic either way.
Times shown in Google
Each small bar is one month. Taller bars mean Google showed the site more often. Being shown is not the same as being clicked.
Google page (Oct avg.)
Which page of Google's results the site usually appears on (10 results per page). Page 1 gets most clicks, page 2 a few, page 3 or beyond very few. Lower is better. This is an average across all searches.
Pages in Google
How many of the site's pages Google has chosen to keep in its results, for example 14% (59 of 413). Pages left out can't be found. Higher is better; 50% or more is strong. The total includes tag pages, redirects and old links, so no site reaches 100%.
